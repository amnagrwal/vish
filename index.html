01 · The Beginning

The day I met you

There are some people who enter our lives and become important immediately. And then there are people who enter quietly, without us having any idea how much they are going to mean to us.

I think you were the second kind.

We met in 2015, during graduation, when we were simply two students living through our own little world. I didn't know then that the girl I was meeting would eventually become my closest friend, the person I would fall in love with, and finally, the person I would choose to spend my life with.

Looking back, I think the most beautiful part of our story is that we didn't know it was a love story while we were living it.

Every forever has a beginning. Ours began with a friendship.
02 · The Years That Brought Us Closer

Somewhere along the way

By 2018, we were doing our postgraduation together, and by then you were no longer just someone I happened to know. You had become a familiar and important part of my everyday life.

We were growing, learning, figuring out life and becoming the people we are today. And somewhere in between all those ordinary days, something changed.

I can't point to one particular moment and say, that's when I fell in love with you. Maybe there wasn't one. Maybe it was hidden in hundreds of little moments — the conversations, the laughter, the comfort, and the feeling that life was a little better when you were there.

I suppose I didn't suddenly fall in love with you.
I slowly realized I already had.
03 · About Her

The little things

I love the way you smile when you're genuinely happy.

I love the little things you do without realizing I'm watching, and the way your random conversations somehow become memories I want to keep.

I love how you can make an ordinary day feel special just by being part of it. I love your cute anger, the expressions you make, the things you say without thinking, and all those tiny habits that have become so familiar to me.

But perhaps what I love most is the comfort of simply being around you. Sometimes, just having you beside me is enough.

After all these years, there are still things about you that make me smile for no particular reason.

And honestly, I could keep writing about them… but then this magazine would never end.

04 · The Engagement

19 April 2026

There are moments in life that make you stop and appreciate everything that came before them.

19 April 2026 was one of those moments.

After all the years of friendship, growing together, falling in love and building memories, we took another step toward the life we've imagined together.

We got engaged.

My best friend became my love.
My love became my fiancée.
And now, forever has a date.

05 · Her Birthday

Today is about you

Today isn't about our story, our engagement, or even the future we're so excited about. Today is simply about celebrating you.

The girl who walked into my life years ago and, without either of us realizing it, became such an important part of it.

I hope you continue to laugh loudly, dream fearlessly, and remain completely yourself. I wish you a year filled with everything that makes your heart happy.

Happy Birthday, Vishakha. ❤️

06 · The Future

I don't want just a chapter

We've already lived so many beautiful chapters together. We started as friends, became boyfriend and girlfriend, and on 19 April 2026, became fiancé and fiancée.

And on 12 December 2026, we'll begin another one.

But that's not where I want our story to end.

When I imagine our future, I don't only see the wedding day. I see the ordinary mornings, crazy adventures, arguments we'll eventually laugh about, celebrations, quiet evenings, the family we'll build, and the dreams we'll chase together.

I want the life that happens in between the big moments.

Because I don't want just a chapter with you.
I want the whole book.
